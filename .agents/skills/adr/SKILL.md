---
name: adr
description: Load at task kickoff to identify architecture decisions, draft ADRs, and request human sign-off.
---

- At task kickoff, identify decisions that need an ADR. If there are none, proceed.
- Draft one ADR per decision using the repo’s ADR format. Mark it Proposed and record the context, recommendation, rationale, consequences, and viable alternatives.
- Wait for explicit human approval of each ADR before marking it Accepted or implementing the decision. If the proposal changes materially, ask again.
- End the approval request with the bullets below, once per ADR. Include every fact the human needs to decide. Focus especially on trade-offs and consequences of the recommendation.

  - **ADR** — [Title](path/to/adr.md)
  - **Context** — Why this decision is needed now and what constrains it.
  - **Recommend** — What to choose and why.
  - **Consequences** — Main upside, downside, and follow-up.
  - **Alternatives** — Viable options and why each falls short.
  - **Sign-off** — Approve, request changes, or choose an alternative?
